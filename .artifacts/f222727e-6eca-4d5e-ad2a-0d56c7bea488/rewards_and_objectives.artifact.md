## 3. Competitive & Special Rewards

### Tournament Prizes
Tournaments offer dynamic rewards based on the event configuration.
- **Top Finishers**: Can earn large amounts of **Coins**, **XP**, exclusive **Badges**, and **Titles**.
- **Participation**: Often provides a base XP reward even without a top-tier finish.

### Pro Mode Benefits
Users with a **Soteria Pro** subscription (₦15,000/month) or active Pro status receive:
- **Daily Free Games**: 5 free Pro Mode games per day.
- **XP Multipliers**: Increased XP gain from various activities (e.g., 2.5x in tournaments).
- **Exclusive Rewards**: Access to premium store items and special milestones.

### Gameplay Bonuses (Points)
Points earned during matches contribute to leaderboard rankings.
- **Perfect Round**: +500 points for 100% accuracy in a session.
- **Streak Bonus**: +10 points per consecutive correct answer.

---

## 4. Non-Gameplay Earning Methods
Ways to acquire resources without directly playing matches or answering questions.

- **Store Purchases**: Users can buy **Coin Packs** and **Token Packs** directly using local currency (NGN).
- **Pro Subscription**: Monthly subscription providing recurring benefits and daily free entries.
- **Welcome Bonus**: Immediate 50 coin credit upon account creation.
